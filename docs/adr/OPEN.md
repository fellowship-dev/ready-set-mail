| **DMARC discovery: tree walk and `psd=` (RFC 9989) instead of the Public Suffix List** | The PSL decides the organizational domain everywhere (`organizationalDomain` in `packages/dns-checks/src/org-domain.ts`; `lookupDmarc` asks the name, then its organizational domain). `psd=`, a record at a name above the organizational domain, and names of more than eight labels are not read. No major receiver has announced tree-walk support (Google and Microsoft still document RFC 7489; a third-party tracker lists all three majors as unknown, 2026-10-01), so the PSL answer matches what receivers do today. The answers differ only for a record with `psd=n` or `psd=y`, a PSL private-suffix operator that publishes a record without `psd=y`, a name with more than eight labels, and a suffix newer than the bundled `tldts` snapshot. | Keep the PSL until a major receiver reports the discovery method "treewalk" in its aggregate reports (RFC 9990 section 3.1.1.5; the parser does not keep that field yet: a one-line parse-and-count decides it), then implement the tree walk beside it, in one module. |
